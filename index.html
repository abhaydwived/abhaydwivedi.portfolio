ABOUT

I work at the intersection of robotics, reinforcement learning, and intelligent control, with a focus on robot learning, bipedal locomotion, motion planning, and simulation-based control.

My work spans from robot modeling and inverse kinematics to reinforcement learning, trajectory generation, and control, with experience in both simulation and physical robotic systems.
